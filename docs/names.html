Names

Latin declines names (Hieronymo, Plinii, Geuhario); the English uses one form for each person or work. Classical and church authors have their usual English name; Arabic and Jewish authors and works keep Bochart's form, with an identification that a program adds at the first use. Basis: standard; context; general knowledge = likely, not checked in a catalogue; guess = check.
