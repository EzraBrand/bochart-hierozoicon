Samuel Bochart, Hierozoicon

Hierozoicon, sive bipertitum opus de animalibus Sacrae Scripturae (London 1663): the standard early-modern study of every animal in the Bible, in Latin with Greek, Hebrew, Aramaic, Syriac and much Arabic, and a main source for later work on the animals of the Bible and the Talmud. No modern translation exists. This project gives it chapter by chapter, in the edition of E. F. C. Rosenmüller (Leipzig 1793–96), as a machine transcription and English translation. Done so far: 44 chapters, vol. 2, pp. 409–786. Find a verse in the index of Bible passages, or a word in the search.

What this is

  • The transcription and the translation are machine output (Claude Opus 5.5). Every word where a second reader (Gemini Flash, the MDZ OCR) differs was judged on the scan, and the changes were read by eye; three pages (II, 410, 422, 428) were checked by hand.
  • Bochart's citations: Bible references in modern form; classical references with Bochart's own book and chapter numbers; “p. 1012” in Rosenmüller's notes is a column of 1663.
  • Abbreviations and names are identified from tables reviewed by hand (abbreviations, names); “general knowledge” or “guess” marks an identification that is not certain.
  • Each page links to the scan: please check it before citing anything.
  • Code, data, method and costs: github.com/EzraBrand/bochart-hierozoicon.
