# Part 2, Book 3, chapter 11: It is shown that the word קפוז kippoz (Isa 34:15) means neither the hedgehog, as the ancients held, nor a kind of bird, as more recent writers held, but the serpent acontias, or iaculus (vol. 3, pp. 194–205)
